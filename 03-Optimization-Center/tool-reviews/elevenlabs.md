**Terms used below**
- **TTS (text-to-speech):** software that reads text aloud in a synthetic voice.
- **Credits:** ElevenLabs' usage currency. For TTS, roughly **1 credit per character** of script.
- **Voice Library:** a catalogue of voices that other users shared. You pick one and generate with it.
- **Voice Design:** you describe a voice in words (e.g. "low, tired, melancholy male narrator") and it invents one that belongs to no real person.
- **Voice cloning:** making a copy of a real person's voice from recordings. *Instant* clones need a short sample; *Professional* clones need longer recordings plus a verification step.
- **Commercial license:** permission to use the audio in content that earns money, such as a monetized channel.
- **C2PA / Content Credentials:** a hidden label inside a media file that says "made with AI". Platforms can read it and add an AI label automatically.

**Reasoning for the verdict:**
ElevenLabs is the right *kind* of tool for your brief, and it's cheap. But your current bottleneck isn't the voice. Your last session ended with "the video is unusable; decide a realistic visual format first", and the current lesson is 4.4 Evals. Paying for a voice before you can produce a postable video means paying for credits you won't use. The order: finish 4.4, choose the visual format, then audition voices on the **free plan** (testing only, nothing posted), then buy **Starter** in the same week you're ready to publish your first video.

**What would change the verdict:**
- **Implement now (Starter):** you've chosen a visual format that you can produce, and a first video is ready apart from the voice.
- **Switch to CapCut TTS:** you decide the channel's identity doesn't depend on a distinctive voice and you want zero cost, but only after reading CapCut's actual terms.
- **Re-review:** if ElevenLabs changes plan prices or licence terms, or if a voice you rely on gets a removal notice. Prefer a **Voice Design** voice, which belongs to you and can't be pulled by a Library owner.
