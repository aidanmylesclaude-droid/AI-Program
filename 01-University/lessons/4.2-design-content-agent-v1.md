## What you'll learn
How to design an agent on paper before writing code: one job, its inputs and outputs, its tools, its never-do list, its done check, and where you approve.

## Key terms
- **Design doc:** a one-page plan of what the agent does and doesn't do. Code is written from it, and tests check against it.
- **Scope:** the edges of the job. A small scope is easier to build, test and trust.
- **Niche:** the topic area your content is about.
- **Hook:** the first line or two of a video, written to make people keep watching.
- **Server tool (web search):** a tool Anthropic runs for the model (it searches the web and returns results). Costs a small fee per search on top of tokens.

## The problem this solves
"Build me a content agent" is too big to build, test or trust. Most failed agents fail at design: unclear job, too many tools, no definition of done.

## The design template (six questions)
1. **Job:** one sentence. What does it produce?
2. **Inputs:** what you give it each run.
3. **Output:** exactly what comes out, and where it's saved.
4. **Tools:** the fewest that do the job (least privilege).
5. **Never-do list:** enforced by not giving it the tools, plus platform rules.
6. **Done check + approval:** how you know it worked, and what needs your OK.

## Recommended v1 (draft; final version, with the user's choices, is in DESIGN.md)
*User chose: TikTok; niche = myth-busting (factoids, zombie facts, old wives' tales, urban legends, pseudoscience); job = 5 myths + sourced truth + hooks.*
- **Job (original draft):** "Find 5 current video ideas in my niche and draft a hook for each."
- **Inputs:** niche, platform, (optional) past videos that did well.
- **Output:** one Markdown file in the vault, `content-agent/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-ideas.md`.
- **Tools:** web search (read-only), save-draft (writes only inside `drafts/`).
- **Never:** post, comment, DM, log in to accounts, copy others' content word for word, fake engagement, run multiple accounts.
- **Done check:** 5 ideas; each has a source link and date; hook ≤ 2 sentences; flags where AI disclosure would apply.
- **Approval:** everything it makes is a **draft**. You decide what to film or post.
- **Four checks (1.6.1):** complexity ✓ (search + judge + write), value ✓, viability ✓, cost of error ✓ (drafts only; nothing public).

- Prediction: **one** leg (untrusted content from web search). No private data, no way to send or post anything out.
1. Least privilege + approval: posting is public and hard to undo, and v1's job doesn't need it. Drafts let you check everything first.
